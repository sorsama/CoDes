# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and technical teams who run multiple local coding-agent CLIs while working in real repositories. They need to understand what each agent is doing, retain control over permissions, and move work from planning through implementation and verification without manually copying context between terminals.

## Product Purpose

CoDes is a local-first desktop workspace for configuring, launching, observing, and coordinating coding-agent CLIs. Success means users can trust the displayed state, customize their workflow, recover interrupted work, and inspect the real terminal evidence behind every result.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and transparent. CoDes should feel as approachable as a polished native productivity tool while retaining the precision expected from a developer environment.

## Anti-references

Do not imitate noisy operations dashboards, decorative sci-fi terminals, glass-heavy control panels, fake progress meters, or demo interfaces that claim work succeeded without provider and test evidence.

## Design Principles

- Make orchestration understandable at a glance without hiding the underlying terminal.
- Show truthful state, exact progress, actionable blockers, and durable artifacts.
- Keep advanced controls available through progressive disclosure.
- Reuse familiar desktop productivity patterns and the existing CoDes visual vocabulary.
- Make recovery, cancellation, permissions, and failure handling explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic status labels, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, screen-reader-friendly controls, and reduced-motion preferences.
